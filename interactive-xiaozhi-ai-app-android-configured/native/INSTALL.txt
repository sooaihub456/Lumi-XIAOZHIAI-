MORI ANDROID PREVIEW

1. Download mori-preview.apk to your Android phone.
2. If you downloaded the Actions artifact ZIP, extract it first.
3. Tap mori-preview.apk. If Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to
   install unknown apps, then confirm Install. You can turn that permission
   off again immediately afterward. Keep Play Protect enabled.
4. Open Mori. The companion, worlds, gestures, and local-preview chat work
   without a Xiaozhi account. Allow microphone permission only if you want
   to use voice input.

REQUIREMENTS
Android 7.0 or newer, with Android System WebView or Chrome 120 or newer.
Use a recent device for the best 3D performance.
Voice recognition needs an installed Android speech service and may need
internet. Demo speech needs an installed English text-to-speech voice.

LIVE XIAOZHI
The APK does not include an AI server or private credentials. Run the Mori
bridge on a server with HTTPS/WSS, include https://localhost in its
ALLOWED_ORIGINS, then enter its wss:// address and your paired credentials
in Settings & connection. localhost on your phone is the phone itself,
not your computer. Do not disable TLS checks to connect.

TEST BUILD
This APK is debug-signed for personal testing. Do not submit it to the
Play Store or use it as a production distribution. No credentials are
bundled. The SHA-256 checksum is included alongside the APK.

UPDATING
GitHub Actions caches its test signing key. If the key changes (for example,
after cache eviction), Android may refuse to update an older installation.
Export your conversations first; uninstalling Mori removes local app data.
Then uninstall the old test app and install the new APK.
